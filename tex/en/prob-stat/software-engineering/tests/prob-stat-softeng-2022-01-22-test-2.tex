\begin{problem}
Human height, as an outcome of natural processes, can be modelled well by
a normal distribution. The data used here give a mean height of 163 cm and standard deviation 5.6 cm for women in Bulgaria.

Choose a random group of 20 women.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item (0.5 points) What is the probability that their average height exceeds 165 cm?
    \item (0.5 points) What is the probability that the group includes someone taller than 180 cm?
\end{enumerate}

\end{problem}


\begin{problem}
Let $X,Y$ have joint density $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=ce^{-y}$ for $0<x<y<\infty$, and zero elsewhere, where $c$ is
a constant.

\begin{itemize}
    \item (0.75 points) Find $c$ and $Cor(X,Y)$.
\item (0.25 points) Find $\E(X|Y=1)$.

\end{itemize}


\end{problem}


\begin{problem} (1 point)

An enthusiast wants to estimate $\pi$ using a computer without internet access. They simulate many uniformly distributed points in $[0,1]\times[0,1]$ and examine the
proportion falling inside the circle inscribed in the square.

Explain how and why this estimates $\pi$. How many points must
be simulated so that the probability of an error below 0.001 is 95\%?
\end{problem}

    for arbitrary continuous $X,Y$?
    \item (0.75 points) Find the densities of $X/Y$ and $Y/X$. \textit{(Bonus: Compare their expectations.)}
\end{enumerate}

\end{problem}
